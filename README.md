## ⚡ Who Am I

I don't wait to be taught — I build to learn.

While most people read about AI and game development, I'm the one actually shipping it. I've gone from zero to building **multiplayer fighting games with real-time networking**, **AI-powered educational video generators**, **smart agriculture apps** that serve actual farmers, and **3D game engines you can control with your voice**. Every project I've built starts with a single question: *"Why doesn't this exist yet?"*

I operate at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence**, **Game Development**, and **Mobile & Web Engineering**. I don't specialize in just one domain — I cross boundaries. One week I'm writing custom physics engines in Kotlin for an Android fighting game. The next week I'm wiring together Groq, Gemini, MoviePy, and gTTS to build an AI that turns any topic into a full video lesson with dual-voice narration.

What drives me is **impact at scale** — building software that real people use, whether it's a bus passenger needing live route help, a farmer checking market prices, or a student who learns better through visual storytelling.

I believe the best developers aren't the ones who memorize the most syntax — they're the ones who can **see the problem clearly and build the solution fearlessly**.
